Privacy notice prototype

How appointment information is handled

This prototype collects only the information a patient chooses to submit for an appointment request or payment. In demo mode, form entries are stored only in the user’s browser and may be cleared at any time.

Production requirements

Before launch, the clinic should publish its final privacy notice, identify the legal entity controlling the data, define retention periods, restrict staff access, secure the database and provide a process for correction or deletion requests.

Payment information

Card or online banking credentials should be entered only on the licensed payment provider’s secure page. The clinic website should store transaction references and status, not banking credentials.

Medical information

Do not use this public form for emergencies or highly sensitive medical details. The clinic should move clinical history into its approved patient record workflow.
