Why the doctor checks first

Not every small growth is a skin tag

Warts, moles, seborrhoeic keratoses, milia and other lesions can look similar. A changing, bleeding, painful or unusually pigmented lesion may need a different evaluation before any cosmetic removal.

Removal options may include

  • Simple snip or excision for selected lesions
  • Electrocautery or other energy-based removal where available
  • Cryotherapy for suitable lesions
  • Referral or laboratory testing when the lesion appears atypical
The final treatment offered depends on the doctor’s assessment, the clinic’s registered scope, available equipment and the practitioner’s credentials.

Questions before removal

Any procedure can leave a mark. The risk varies with the lesion, method, skin type, healing and aftercare. Results cannot be guaranteed.

Home cutting or burning can cause bleeding, infection, scarring or treatment of the wrong lesion. Medical assessment is the safer approach.
