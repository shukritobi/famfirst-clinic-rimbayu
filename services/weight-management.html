Doctor assessment required

Your first visit may include

  • Weight, BMI and blood pressure review

  • Discussion of sleep, diet, activity and stress
  • Review of relevant blood tests, when indicated
  • Personalised goals and follow-up schedule
Why medical assessment matters

Weight is not only about willpower

Difficulty losing weight can be influenced by eating patterns, sleep, activity, medical conditions, medications, hormones, stress and the environment around you. A proper consultation helps separate assumptions from factors that can actually be addressed.

What the programme should focus on

  • Realistic targets based on your starting health and daily routine
  • Malaysian meal patterns and practical portion strategies
  • Movement that fits your current fitness and joint health
  • Monitoring of weight, waist, blood pressure and relevant lab results
  • Medication discussion only when clinically suitable and legally appropriate
No medication, injection or supplement is suitable for everyone. The doctor decides after reviewing benefits, risks, contraindications and the need for monitoring.

Weight management FAQ

Questions patients commonly ask

Not every patient needs the same tests. The doctor may recommend tests based on symptoms, medical history, risk factors and previous results.

Medication is not automatic. It is considered only when the doctor determines that it is suitable, safe and likely to add benefit alongside lifestyle changes.

The safe and realistic rate varies. The programme should prioritise health improvement, adherence and maintaining results instead of promising a fixed number of kilograms.

Weight management can be relevant to several metabolic conditions, but diagnosis and treatment need an individual medical assessment. Bring any recent blood results and medication list.
