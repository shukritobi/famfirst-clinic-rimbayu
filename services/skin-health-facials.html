Medical facial positioning

Skin care begins with understanding the skin

A facial treatment should not be selected only by trend or package name. Active acne, eczema, infection, medication use, pregnancy and skin sensitivity can change what is suitable.

A good consultation should cover

  • Your current skin products and any recent reactions
  • Duration, triggers and previous treatment for the concern
  • Medical conditions, pregnancy status and medications
  • Realistic goals, downtime and aftercare
  • Whether a medical facial, prescription, procedure or referral is more appropriate
